### Architecture Rating Section

merit-v4 supersedes merit-v3.1 rating methodology. Historical ratings now combine individual tournament merit with a public national-team-strength prior: World Football Elo all-years plus official FIFA ranking snapshots from 1994 onward. The prior replaces the old nation-blind raw-only `0.62` ceiling with a smooth `(tournament, nation)` ceiling range from `0.500` to `0.625`; awards and material career stature can still overrun it.

The `club_honors` input family is active for public-factual internal inputs: major club trophies, continental club titles, league top-scorer by goals, and world-record/era-defining transfer facts. Fan votes, proprietary ratings, subjective club/player-of-year routes, EA/FC/FIFA-style ratings, and inferred/fabricated facts remain excluded.

The merit-v3.1 88-wall STOP is retired because merit-v4 changed the internal layer. Exact `raw_only_ceiling == 0.62` went `10,735 -> 0`; exact `raw_only_score == 0.62` went `1,162 -> 0`. The old pigeonhole conflict with fixed median/control constraints no longer binds as a release blocker. Keep the note as historical design reasoning rather than deleting it.
