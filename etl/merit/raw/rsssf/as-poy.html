Asian Player of the Year

Officially awarded since 1994. The official name is Asian Football Confederation's Sanyo Player of the Year Award. Prior to 1994 it was awarded on an unofficial basis. The winners from 1988 to 1991 were chosen by IFFHS.

NB: Premier Publications organised a poll in 1984 to mark the first edition of their Asia-Oceania Soccer Handbook.
